Emergency help

If you or someone near you is in danger, do not use the chat. Call for help right now.

112 National emergency numberPolice, fire and ambulance 108 AmbulanceFree medical emergency service in most states

Helpline numbers can change over time. 112 is meant to work all over India.

Get help straight away if you have

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Bleeding that will not stop
  • Fainting or loss of consciousness
  • Sudden weakness, a drooping face or slurred speech
  • Swelling of the face or throat, or a severe allergic reaction
  • A seizure, or sudden confusion
  • Thoughts of harming yourself
