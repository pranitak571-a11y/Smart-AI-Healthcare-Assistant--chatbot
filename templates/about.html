About this assistant

A college project that gives general, first-step guidance about common health symptoms. It is not a doctor.

How it works

  1. You describe how you feel. Type in English, Marathi or Hindi, or tap the + button for quick symptoms.
  2. It picks out the symptoms it knows. It also checks for signs of an emergency.
  3. It compares them with common conditions and ranks the closest matches.
  4. You get a short answer: possible conditions, a basic self-care tip and a reminder to see a doctor.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot diagnose. Its suggestions are only possibilities and can be wrong.
  • It covers common, non-emergency conditions only.
  • It does not recommend medicines or doses.
  • It is not for emergencies. See the Emergency help page.

Your data

  • Your password is stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text.
  • Your chat is saved to your account so you can continue later. Clear chat removes it from your history.
  • If you press the thumbs up or down, that message and reply are saved with your rating, to help improve the tool.
  • The dashboard only keeps counts, not your messages.

Always consult a licensed medical professional about your health.
